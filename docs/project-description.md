**Design and Development of an AI-Enabled Smart Electric Vehicle Charging and Energy Management Platform**

*Supervisor:* Dr Amer Ghias | *Affiliation:* University of Glasgow (Glasgow College UESTC)

---

## 1. Background

The rapid growth of electric vehicle (EV) adoption is increasing the demand for charging
infrastructure and placing additional pressure on electricity networks. Uncoordinated EV
charging can result in increased peak demand, higher electricity costs, and inefficient
utilization of available energy resources. Furthermore, the increasing penetration of renewable
energy sources such as solar photovoltaic systems introduces additional challenges and
opportunities for intelligent charging management.

Recent advances in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, and data analytics have
enabled the development of smart charging systems capable of predicting charging demand,
optimizing charging schedules, and improving overall energy efficiency. Intelligent EV charging
platforms can help charging operators and users reduce charging costs, minimize peak demand,
increase renewable energy utilization, and support sustainable transportation objectives.

This project aims to design and develop an AI-enabled smart electric vehicle charging and energy
management platform using publicly available datasets, open-source software tools, and
established engineering methods. The platform will combine charging demand forecasting, smart
charging scheduling, renewable-aware charging strategies, and real-time monitoring to improve
the performance and efficiency of EV charging infrastructure.

## 2. Main Tasks

The project will be undertaken by five students, each responsible for an independent subsystem
while contributing to the overall platform development.

### Student 1 — EV Charging Data Analysis and Demand Forecasting
- Collect and preprocess public EV charging datasets.
- Analyze charging patterns and user behavior.
- Develop machine learning models to predict future charging demand.
- Evaluate forecasting accuracy and model performance.

### Student 2 — Smart Charging Scheduling
- Investigate existing charging strategies and electricity tariff structures.
- Develop charging scheduling algorithms that minimize charging costs and peak demand.
- Compare uncontrolled and intelligent charging approaches.
- Evaluate scheduling performance under different operating scenarios.

### Student 3 — Renewable-Aware Charging Management
- Model solar photovoltaic generation using public datasets.
- Develop charging strategies that maximize renewable energy utilization.
- Analyze the impact of renewable integration on charging performance and energy consumption.

### Student 4 — Charging Monitoring and Visualisation Platform
- Design a charging monitoring architecture.
- Develop a software dashboard to display charging status, energy consumption, and charging costs.
- Implement data visualization and reporting functions.

### Student 5 — Economic, Environmental and System Assessment
- Assess charging cost savings achieved by the proposed platform.
- Evaluate carbon emission reduction and environmental benefits.
- Conduct overall system performance evaluation and benchmarking.
- Analyze commercial feasibility and user benefits.

## 3. Expected Outcomes

The project is expected to deliver an integrated platform capable of supporting intelligent
decision-making in its domain. Expected outcomes include:

- EV charging dataset repository and data analysis framework.
- Machine learning-based charging demand forecasting model.
- Smart charging scheduling algorithms for cost and peak-demand reduction.
- Renewable-aware charging management module.
- Charging monitoring and visualization dashboard.
- Economic, environmental, and sustainability assessment reports.
- Integrated software prototype demonstrating intelligent EV charging management.
- Final technical report, dissertation, and project presentation.
